Lens for Browsers — Settings

Selection button

A small Lens dot next to text you select, which opens Lens Mini right on the page. It never appears on banking, payment or sign-in pages, on pages with a visible password field, or in password and sensitive fields.

Sites with the selection button

    Default action

    Highlighted in the in-page panel; press Enter to run it.

    Never on these sites

    Lens never shows the selection button here, even when it's on for all sites.

      Built-in list (always off)

      Clean copy

      Links you copy with Lens lose their tracking parameters (utm_…, fbclid, gclid and similar). This setting also stops sites changing what you copy, such as adding "Read more at…" or blocking copy. It doesn't apply on banking or sign-in sites, or on sites you list under "Never".

      Double-click a word

      Double-click a word to see a short definition or translation next to it. Answers come only from a local model in Lens (Ollama or LM Studio). Nothing is sent to the cloud; without a local model, the bubble says so. Lens doesn't include an offline dictionary yet.

      Privacy

      This extension makes no network requests of its own. It talks only to Lens on this PC through the browser's native messaging. The one exception is "Get transcript", which asks YouTube for the caption track its own player uses. Answers come from a local model unless you let Lens use Claude, and every cloud request waits for your OK in Lens.
