Why calm software wins

Most tools compete for attention. The best ones give it back. Calm software keeps you in flow: it asks for attention only when it matters, picks sensible defaults, and gets out of the way.

Fewer, better-timed notifications beat clever features. When a tool respects your focus, you trust it with more of your work — and that trust compounds over months of daily use.

Defaults matter more than options. Every setting is a question the user has to answer; good defaults answer most of them before they're asked.

Context switching is expensive. A desktop that brings the right thing to you — a summary, a file, a reminder — saves more time than one that makes everything one click away.
