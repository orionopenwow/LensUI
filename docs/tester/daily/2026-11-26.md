## What changed

### Dock
- **Menus close properly.** The Dock's own buttons now open and close their menus: press the gear once to open, and again to close. Settings and Search have a visible ×. A quick click elsewhere reliably closes Search or a Dock menu (#102, #83).
- **Window previews on hover are on by default,** as on Windows. If you turned them off before, they come on once; switching them off again sticks (#102).
- **Smoother Dock.** Magnification, hover and reorder respond faster. A thin white line that flashed while Search opened or the style changed is gone (#83, #102).
- **Menus close when another app comes to the front,** even with the pointer still over the menu (#102).
- Also new since 24 Nov:
  - Dock styles no longer change the width of the drop-up menus.
  - The Taskbar style opens Settings and Search like the Windows 11 Start menu (#95).
  - Previews no longer get stuck in the bottom-right corner (#79).
  - The Taskbar style has Windows 11's flat hover plates (#72).

### Themes
- **First-run setup starts from your own desktop:** your light or dark mode, your wallpaper's colour and the nearest look, all pre-selected. Nothing changes until you press Finish; Skip changes nothing (#99, #85).
- **Lens's accent is only copied into Windows** if you tick "Also use this accent in Windows", which is off by default (#102).
- Also new since 24 Nov:
  - Switching themes in Settings is clean and instant, with no growing or flashing (#88).
  - Themes change colours only; they no longer change your Dock style (#95).

### Windows
- **The red, yellow and green window dots fully cover the real Windows buttons.** The native Close button no longer lights up or shows its tooltip underneath. The dots follow the window through move, maximise, snap and restore. Resting on the green dot opens Snap Layouts (#99).
- Also new since 24 Nov: the dots blend into every title bar, including gradient and maximised ones (#80).

### Desktop
- **Safer sign-in.** With "Start with Windows" on, Lens waits until your desktop and taskbar are really there, and never starts on the lock or sign-in screen. **Hold Shift while signing in to skip Lens** (#101).
- **The keys Windows needs always work:** Ctrl+Alt+Del, Ctrl+Shift+Esc, Alt+F4, Win+L, Win+D and Win+Tab (#101).
- **Click through the Top Bar.** Only the bar's own buttons take clicks. Anywhere else, the click goes to the app underneath, such as browser tabs or title bars. A soft see-through halo follows the pointer (#102).
- **The Dock and Top Bar move to the right place** when you plug in or unplug a monitor, change resolution or scaling, or wake from sleep. There is a new "Follow the pointer" monitor choice (#101).
- **Pop-outs close by themselves** (Quick Settings, Settings on the Dock) about 1.5 seconds after the pointer leaves. You can switch this off (#101).
- **If Lens gets stuck, the Dock and Top Bar become click-through after 2 seconds,** so they can't block other apps (#101).
- **The auto-hidden Top Bar no longer pops back up and stays** after a settings save or Search (#94).
- Also new since 24 Nov:
  - Start with Windows is off for new installs (#76).
  - "Remove all" works on a fresh PC (#93) and keeps your Lens data if an undo step fails (#76).

### AI and Command
- Also new since 24 Nov:
  - The AI is now just called "Lens" everywhere (#71).
  - Feeds and Messages are reachable (#71).
  - Magic opens from Dock apps (Alt+right-click), Shelf items and notifications (#72).
  - One clipboard history (#70).
- **Right-click "Lens ›" row:** in Lens's right-click menus, one row with the Lens logo slides into the Magic actions. File Explorer has a Lens menu inside folders and on drives (#84).

## Please test today (highest risk first)

1. **Sign-in with Lens starting automatically.** Turn on Settings › Quick › Start with Windows, then sign out and back in.
   - **Expected:** no error windows. The Dock appears only after your normal desktop and taskbar are up, and nothing shows on the lock screen.
   - Repeat while holding **Shift** at sign-in. **Expected:** Lens does not start.
2. **Top Bar click-through.** Put a browser window right under the Top Bar and click its tabs and title bar through the bar.
   - **Expected:** the browser gets the click, and the bar's own buttons (clock, Quick Settings, Search) still work.
   - **Expected:** there are no dead zones. Note anything that can't be clicked.
3. **Window dots.** Open Notepad and hover the top-right corner. Then maximise, snap (Win+←), restore and resize it.
   - **Expected:** the native Close button never turns red and its tooltip never shows. The dots stay over the corner, and briefly hide during size changes.
   - Rest on the green dot. **Expected:** Snap Layouts opens.
4. **Monitors.** With two screens, unplug one or change a screen's resolution or scaling. Sleep and wake the PC. Then try Settings › Dock & Bar › Monitor › Follow the pointer.
   - **Expected:** the Dock and Top Bar end up on a real screen, in the right place.
   - **Expected:** with Follow the pointer, they move when you push against a screen edge, not on every crossing.
5. **Protected keys.** While a Lens menu is open, press Ctrl+Shift+Esc, Alt+F4, Win+D and Win+L.
   - **Expected:** Windows reacts every time.
6. **Dock buttons and close buttons.** Press the Dock's gear twice, the Top Bar's Quick Settings twice, then open Search and press × and Esc.
   - **Expected:** a second press closes. × and Esc close Search wherever the focus is. Nothing reopens by itself.
7. **Auto-hide Top Bar.** Set the Top Bar to auto-hide. While it is hidden, change any setting, then open and close Search.
   - **Expected:** the bar hides again and doesn't stay stuck on screen.
8. **Pop-outs auto-close.** Open Quick Settings from the Dock and move the pointer away.
   - **Expected:** it closes after about 1.5 seconds. It doesn't close while the pointer is over it or a button is held down.
9. **Window previews.** Hover a running app on the Dock.
   - **Expected:** live previews appear next to the Dock and disappear shortly after you leave.
   - **Expected:** no previews are stuck in a screen corner.
10. **First-run setup.** Settings › About & Testing › Setup.
    - **Expected:** it opens in your desktop's colours, with a look pre-selected.
    - Press **Skip**. **Expected:** nothing changes.
    - Run it again and press **Finish**. **Expected:** only what you picked changes.
11. **Lens Browser permissions.** Open a site that asks for your camera or location (for example a video-call test page).
    - **Expected:** a Lens bar appears under the address bar, and Esc blocks.
    - "Allow on this site" is remembered, and Settings › AI & Command › Lens Browser can forget it.
12. **Windows accent.** Pick a new Lens accent with "Also use this accent in Windows" off.
    - **Expected:** Windows' own accent colour does not change.
    - Tick the box. **Expected:** Windows' accent now matches Lens.

## Known issues

- If a window's renderer crashes during start-up, Lens may freeze for up to a minute before it recovers (B-31b). This has only been seen in automated tests. Note it if it happens.
- Lens's File Explorer entries are under **Show more options** (Shift+F10) on Windows 11. On PCs with the new right-click "Customize menu" (optional update KB5127936), hiding "app extensions" may hide them. Please report what you see.
- The desktop tab and the edge Shelf may still briefly resize their window (B-30).
- One-click transcription setup is blocked until download hashes are pinned (B-07).
- After the November Windows update (KB5132061), some other Windhawk taskbar mods stop loading; turn them off and on again. Lens's own window-style mod isn't affected.
- Choosing another theme pack and then the first one again doesn't restore folder colours (B-39).
- The Top Bar click-through and its halo were checked only by automated tests. Please confirm them on a real PC, together with the Dock gear and Quick Settings toggles.
