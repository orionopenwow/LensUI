## What changed

### Dock
- **Auto-hide no longer gets stuck open**, and clicking anywhere outside the Dock closes its menus (#52).
- The Dock's size and click area now come from what it actually draws, so styles shouldn't clip or leave dead space above the icons (B-15, #62).
- The **Taskbar** dock style uses Windows 11's real taskbar proportions (#68).
- Pinned apps copied from the Windows taskbar **keep Windows' order** until you reorder them in Lens (#49).

### Themes
- **Applying a theme no longer freezes the PC.** The Windows-side work runs in the background and is skipped when nothing changed (#53).
- **Twelve featured looks** that are clearly different from each other: Obsidian, Alabaster Daylight, Sky, Catppuccin Mocha, Hyprland Neon, Cyberpunk Amber, Paper Minimal, Lens Expressive, Lens Dot Matrix, Everforest, Solarized Matte and Gruvbox Warm. The other 77 are under **More themes** (#65).

### Windows
- **Yellow · Green · Red window buttons** option (#60).
- Windows' own open/close animation is turned off for Lens panels, so they glide in without a double animation (#60).

### Desktop
- **Wallpaper Switcher**: a wallpaper picker, a Settings card, and auto-change on a timer with a crossfade. Also reachable from `/wallpaper` in Command, by voice and from the tray (#65).
- **Fixed: the desktop covered by "can't reach this page" windows at sign-in.** "Start with Windows" now refuses development builds and removes old entries that point at one (B-21, #49).

### AI and Command
- **In-page Lens buttons** (off by default): a small Lens orb next to tweets, YouTube, GitHub, videos, images, 3D models, code and downloads, in the browser extension and in Lens Browser (#57).
- **Command's first Enter after start-up**: a race was fixed (#62), and a remaining rare case is now traced (B-12b, #64).

### Browser
- **Lens Browser, big update** (#67): Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+1…9, F5, Ctrl+H, zoom keys; address-bar suggestions from open tabs, bookmarks and history; Back and Forward work properly; Reload becomes Stop while loading; zoom remembered per site; Find with a match count; a new-tab page; history and downloads panels; and importing from other browsers item by item, with a receipt.

### Settings
- **Simple / Advanced** switch: Simple shows the main settings as picture cards, and Advanced shows everything. New users start in Simple (#56).
- **Quick** page at the top of Settings with your most-used toggles, quick theme and window layouts. Tiles can be reordered and hidden (#55).
- **Quick Settings** (#60): brightness for each screen (external monitors too), volume that unmutes when raised, real Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / Display / Sound states, Night light warmth and schedule, and live window thumbnails.

### Under the hood
- **Failsafe** (#66): if one Lens window crashes it reloads itself. After three crashes that part turns off and tells you once. A Lens that keeps crashing at start-up brings your taskbar back and offers **Safe Mode**. A broken setting resets only that setting, not all of them.
- **Remove all Lens changes** now works from one list of everything Lens changes, including Lens Apps setups, the Windows accent colour, workspaces and the live wallpaper. It ends with "Not changed: apps you installed" (#62).
- **One keyboard service** instead of several keyboard hooks (#62).
- **Smoothness check** in the Tester Hub, plus one motion system for animations (#61). Smoother presses and page switches (#54).
- Website and CI work: #48, #50, #51, #58, #59, #68.

## Known issues

- **Command's first Enter after start-up may rarely do nothing** (B-12b). If it happens, press Enter again and note the time.
- **Agent safety isn't yet wired into every agent path** (B-04). The fix is in PR #63, which isn't merged. Don't point the agent at untrusted pages.
- **Esc only stops Lens at work while a Lens window has focus** (B-13); use Ctrl+Alt+Pause. PR #63 fixes this.
- The opt-in injected window-style hook loads a DLL into every app, and Remove all doesn't fully stop it until restart (B-05, B-06). Leave it off unless you're testing it.
- The desktop tab and edge Shelf may still briefly resize their window (B-30).
- Magic menu isn't reachable from the Dock, Shelf or notifications yet (B-11). Feeds and Messages panels are built but not shown yet (B-09). One-click transcription setup refuses to run until download hashes are pinned (B-07).
- **Windhawk after the November Windows update (KB5132061):** some *other* Windhawk taskbar mods stop loading. This is now confirmed (Microsoft didn't publish taskbar symbols). Turning the mod off and on again fixes it. Lens's own window-style mod doesn't depend on those symbols.
