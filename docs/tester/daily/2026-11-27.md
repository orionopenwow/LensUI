## What changed

### Dock
- **New "Unified" Dock style:** one floating pill with the Launcher first, then your apps, then the tray, Quick Settings, a notification bell and a two-line clock. Pick it in Settings › Dock & Bar › Dock style. Floating stays the default (#117).
- **Window marks and a window carousel in every style.** One dash under an icon means one window and three dots mean two or more. Hovering an app with several windows shows a carousel you can scroll, click or arrow through (#117).
- **Smoother Dock changes.** Pinning, unpinning, reordering, opening and closing apps glide instead of jumping. The Launcher marks pinned apps and has Pin/Unpin on right-click (#117).
- **Dock keys.** With a Dock icon focused, use the arrows, Home/End, or type a name to find an app. The arrow that points into the screen opens that app's window list (#137).
- **Dock fixes:** clicks that used to fail silently now show "Couldn't open …". A window run as administrator no longer lands on the wrong pin. Right-click menus stay on screen on side Docks. The icon size menu works on every style (#137).
- **New music pop-out.** Hover Spotify (or another player) in the Dock for artwork, a level meter, a seek bar, bigger controls and a soft "sound glow" in the artwork's colours. You can turn the glow and meter off in Settings › Music (#120).
- **Simpler Lens app icons:** thick white symbols on a plain dark tile. Lens Browser keeps the colourful Lens logo (#139).
- **Dock & Bar › Advanced** collects magnification, launch animation, app names, scroll-to-switch, icon size per style and more in one place (#113).
- With the Dock on the left or right edge, Settings and Quick Settings now open beside it and inside the screen (#119).

### Themes
- **Going back to your first theme choice restores everything,** including folder colours and the Day & night schedule (B-39 fixed) (#113).
- **Themes no longer change your Dock.** Two near-duplicate packs were merged: CRT is now Green Phosphor and Claude Light Brand is now Claude Light. Old choices still work (#113).
- **Every pack was checked for readable text.** 18 dark packs got lighter secondary text, and Everforest Light and Base16 Cupcake got darker accents (#118).
- **Theme switching is quicker,** with no change in how anything looks (#142).
- Backdrop Lab has a new "Lens Cloud" preset (#113).

### Windows
- **Lens's window buttons honour more settings.** Excluded apps, the button style (classic, soft, small dots, pills) and the left/right position now apply to other apps too. The left side has only been checked in tests, so look for buttons that cover icons or titles (#115).
- **Per-app switch:** Settings › Windows & Keys › Window styling lists apps with a switch for Lens's window buttons. Protected apps such as games with anti-cheat show a locked "Protected" badge (#115).
- One shared anti-cheat list is now used everywhere, so Alt+drag can no longer move a protected game's window (#115).

### Desktop
- **Multi-monitor fixes:** the Dock should now appear on a second monitor with different scaling (B-62), and Settings and Search no longer cover the Dock on small high-scaling screens (B-64) (#119).
- **Top Bar auto-hide:** after a hiccup at start-up, an auto-hidden Top Bar no longer stays drawn on screen (B-126). The Launcher now closes when you click outside it (B-128) (#140).
- **Top Bar:** a single on/off switch now controls it, so Settings always matches the screen. Extra bars on other monitors follow "Follow the pointer" correctly (#113). A new "Pointer halo" switch turns the halo off, and the halo also disappears when Windows' transparency effects are off (#118).
- **After an Explorer restart,** the Dock still highlights apps that flash for attention (B-65). Menus and pop-outs no longer stay stuck open after lock/unlock or focus changes (#119).
- **Magic button (off by default):** a quick tap of the Windows key opens Lens Magic Start, with app search, pins, Magic actions, recent files, Lock and a Windows Start button. Ctrl+Esc always opens Windows Start. Esc closes it even right after it opens (#107, #125, #136).
- **Magic Mouse (off by default):** an animated Lens pointer. Hold the left button for about half a second, then drag, to grab part of the screen into Snip. Hold without moving to open Magic. Your normal pointers come back when it's off, when Lens exits or when a game is in front (#109).
- **Desktop widgets (off by default):** macOS-style widgets on your desktop, below your windows: clock, world clock, today, notes, now playing, battery and weather. They sit in a grid and you can add, resize and move them in edit mode (#123).
- **Magic Dashboard (off by default):** a side panel with Inbox, Feeds, Today, Shelf, Now playing, an optional Widgets lane (weather, news, clocks and more, each source approved by you) and an X lane (#104, #108).

### AI and Command
- **Selection bar and Magic Search (off by default).** Select text in almost any app and a small bar offers Cut, Copy, Paste, Magic Search and your actions. Magic Search gives a quick answer with web results and matches from your own Lens files (#128).
- **Approvals are tied to the exact step.** An "OK" now only works for the action it was shown for. If a page changes the button after you approved it, Lens asks again (#114).
- **Plan view:** when Lens proposes several steps at once, you see the whole list and can Allow or Deny each step (#114).
- **Command undo survives a restart** (last 50 steps) (#114).
- **"Teach me":** after Command changes a setting, it says where that setting lives, with a "Show me" link. There are also example commands under the empty Do field (#115).
- **Magic window:** "Snip this window" and "Open in Lens Browser" now appear (B-70) (#136).
- **Shelf:** Undo after Remove, and the Menu key/Shift+F10 opens an item's menu. **Shelf to Notes** (off by default) turns Shelf items into a note or a .md/.txt/PDF file (#136, #107).

### Voice
- A voice conversation no longer leaves listeners behind each time you interrupt it, and its hidden window stays idle (B-120) (#136).

### Browser
- **Reader view:** the toolbar's Reader button shows the page's text cleanly. Size, width and font are remembered (#114).
- A site-permission request that nobody answered for 60 seconds now says so on its tab. Errors from browser buttons now show up instead of failing silently (#114).
- Unread counts from web messengers (Slack, Discord and others) open in Lens Browser now reach the Messages badge on the Dock (#112).

### Snip
- **Live text:** in the Snip studio, press **L** to select words on the image. Copy, Highlight or Hide exactly those words. Links, emails, phone numbers and codes get one-press copy buttons (#136).
- **Snip's default shortcut is now Ctrl+Alt+X** (it used to clash with Save As). A shortcut you chose yourself is kept (#115).
- **Fixes:** cropped-out text no longer sneaks into Copy text. After a crop, the preview isn't stretched and marks land where you draw. Enter on a button only runs that button. Snip history has Undo after Remove (#136).

### Settings
- **Presses feel weighted** (on for everyone). Buttons, switches and chips sink briefly and spring back. Settings pages slide in, and Quick Settings and Search grow from the Top Bar button you pressed. With reduced motion everything is instant (#131).
- **Switches no longer disappear or lose focus after a click** (B-40), and the Hot corners picker can be set up while Hot corners is off (B-41) (#118).
- **Pop-out close delay:** Short, Normal or Long in Dock & Bar › Advanced › Close after (#118).
- **One Notifications page** (history, Feeds and Messages together under Focus & Notifications). "Apps" is renamed "Lens Apps" in the sidebar (#113).
- **Safety net card** in About & Testing lists what Lens touches, what it never touches and the keys that always reach Windows. The Updates card says plainly when updates aren't available yet (#115).
- **Lens apps:**
  - **Lens Music** (Settings › Music, off by default): **Immersive** full-screen Now Playing (F11), artwork-coloured accents, per-song lyric timing (`[` / `]`), opt-in lyric translation, opt-in Discord status and a three-step setup (#127).
  - **Lens Music Equalizer:** switches presets in Equalizer APO if you have it installed. **Lens Remote** lets you control music from your phone, in Tester mode only (#130).
  - **Lens Apps Library:** everything you have, available updates, **Update all** with one approval, and credits and licence notes on every app page (#134).
  - **Alarms** ring at the right time after a daylight-saving or time-zone change, and say when they're late after sleep (B-124, B-125). In Lens Notes, "Remind me" on a to-do sets a Lens Timer (#134).
  - **Cleaner:** permanent delete now needs a typed 4-digit code, then a hold (#112).
  - **Spotify sign-in** works when port 43117 is busy (#112).
  - Opt-in "Lyrics on the desktop" (#112).

### Under the hood
- Many error messages that used to be swallowed now show up, in the Dock, Settings, Lens Apps, Snip and Shelf (B-18, ongoing) (#118, #119, #134, #136, #137).
- Steadier automated Windows tests (Top Bar auto-hide, theme toggle, Control Center) and a fairer theme-switch speed check (#140, #142).
- Lens Mission Control, a developer-only window that is not in tester builds (#133).
- Website updates: Dock-first home page, Dock style picker, smoother demo desk and new screenshots (#106, #116, #121, #135, #138, #141).
- Docs and plans: feature dossiers, the combinator plan and the selection bar plan (#110, #111, #122, #124).

## Please test today (highest risk first)

1. **Weighted presses (on for everyone).** In Settings, Quick Settings (Top Bar) and Search, click buttons, switches, chips and tabs. Click some quickly and hold others. Then turn off Settings › Accessibility › **Animation effects** (Lens then uses reduced motion) and repeat.
   - **Expected:** every press sinks briefly and springs back. One click does one thing, with no double actions and no missed clicks.
   - **Expected:** Quick Settings and Search grow out of the Top Bar button you pressed. With reduced motion there is no movement.
2. **Selection bar and Magic Search (off by default).** Turn on Settings › AI & Command › Selection bar › **Selection bar**. Select text in Notepad, Word, a browser and VS Code by dragging and by double-clicking. Press Copy, then Magic Search. Then select text in a terminal and click into a password field.
   - **Expected:** the bar appears just below the selection without taking the keyboard from the app. Copy puts the text on the clipboard, and Esc hides the bar.
   - **Expected:** Magic Search opens beside the selection and names where the answer came from.
   - **Expected:** no bar in terminals or password fields.
   - The very first selection after start-up may show nothing; see Known issues (B-129).
3. **Top Bar auto-hide right after start-up.** Set Settings › Dock & Bar › Top Bar › Auto-hide to **Always hide**, quit Lens and start it again. Don't touch the top edge for 10 seconds. Then open the Launcher and click on the desktop.
   - **Expected:** the bar hides and stays hidden until the pointer reaches the top edge.
   - **Expected:** the Launcher closes on the click outside.

   - **Expected:** Settings and Quick Settings open beside the Dock and inside the screen.
5. **Magic button (off by default).** With Tester mode on, open Settings › About & Testing › Magic (preview) and turn on **Magic button**. Tap the Windows key, tap it again, then press Esc straight after opening. Press Ctrl+Esc, Win+L, Win+D and Win+E. Turn it off again.

   - Run Remove all Lens changes. **Expected:** the Lens preset line is gone from config.txt.

## Known issues

- **Selection bar:** the first selection after start-up, or the first in a new app, sometimes shows no bar (B-129). The fix is in open PR #144.
- **Selection bar not finished yet:** drag-to-reorder actions, the editor for per-app rules, and the bar's closing animation (it disappears at once). Placement on mixed-scaling monitors has not been checked on a real PC.
- **Magic Mouse:** the automated test for the hold-drag gesture was fixed but has not been re-run. The pointer at 100–200 % scaling and on monitors left of or above the main one has not been checked on a real PC.
- **Desktop widgets:** they only show on Lens's own monitor. Bluetooth device batteries, screen time and photos are not built yet. Widgets don't show remote images yet.
- **Lens Remote** (Tester mode only) uses plain HTTP on your home network, so only use it on a trusted network. Windows may ask about the firewall once; allow Private networks only.
- **Equalizer** needs Equalizer APO installed. Lens can't switch Windows Sonic, so the spatial sound row just opens Sound settings.
- **Window buttons on the left** are drawn only where the title bar is free. On classic title bars they may cover the app icon and the start of the title.
- **There is no hotkey to focus the Dock yet.** Use Alt+Space then Tab, a click, or Alt+1–9.
- Extra Top Bars on other monitors don't auto-hide or click through on their own yet.
- Pinned folders are not drawn on the Dock (not built yet).
- **Cleaner** doesn't review a Downloads folder that has been moved (for example into OneDrive).
- **Updates:** no signed release has been published yet, so "Check now" isn't offered.
- One-click transcription setup is blocked until download hashes are pinned (B-07).
- If a window's renderer crashes during start-up, Lens may freeze for up to a minute before it recovers (B-31b). This has only been seen in automated tests.
- The desktop tab and the edge Shelf may still briefly resize their window (B-30).
- The first Enter in Command after a cold start may occasionally do nothing (B-12b).
- **Settings:** the options under Alt+drag, Magic and App hotkeys can't be clicked while each feature is off (B-58). The fix is in open PR #143.
- Lens's File Explorer entries are under **Show more options** on Windows 11. With the new "Customize menu" (KB5127936), hiding app extensions may hide them.
- Some errors on user actions still fail silently in Quick Settings, Command and Messages (B-18, ongoing).
