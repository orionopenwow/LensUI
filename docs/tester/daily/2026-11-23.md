## Please test today (highest risk first)

1. **Lens Cleaner never deletes without asking** — *Settings › Storage › Open Lens Cleaner* (or `/clean` in Command). Run a scan, open Cleanup, untick everything but one small item, clean it, then press Undo within 30 seconds.
   **Expected:** nothing goes before you click; your own files go to the Recycle Bin; Undo puts the item back exactly where it was.
2. **Remove all Lens changes** — after connecting Claude Desktop (*Settings › AI & Command*) and changing a folder icon, go to *Settings › About › Back up & restore › Remove all Lens changes*, then quit Lens.
   **Expected:** taskbar, pointers, window colours and folder icons are back; the `lens-ui` entry is gone from Claude Desktop's config and a backup sits next to it; no `desktop.ini` you made yourself was deleted.
3. **Lens at work stays out of games and full screen** — ask Lens to do something ("tile my windows"), then start a full-screen game or video while it runs.
   **Expected:** the orb and banner show on the desktop but never over the full-screen app; Ctrl+Alt+Pause stops the run from anywhere (Esc only works when a Lens window has focus, see Known issues).
4. **Living themes** — *Settings › Theme & Look › Living themes*: apply Sky, set your city, drag the time-lapse scrubber, then press "Play a day".
   **Expected:** sunrise, day, dusk and night look right for your city; dock and bar text stay readable all day; turning "Tint Lens with the sky" off restores the pack's colours.
5. **Backdrop Lab live wallpaper** — *Settings › Appearance › Creator › Open Backdrop Lab*. Pick a preset, set it as the live wallpaper, then switch to two other presets and put the PC to sleep and wake it.
   **Expected:** no black or white flash when switching; motion resumes smoothly after sleep; CPU and GPU stay low when a window covers the desktop.
6. **Feeds and Messages never send on their own** — add a Mastodon or Bluesky account, write a post, then let go of the confirm button early.
   **Expected:** nothing is posted unless you hold confirm all the way; the approval expires after about 2 minutes.
7. **Dock styles** — *Settings › Dock › Style*: switch through all six styles; in each, hover and click an app.
   **Expected:** instant change with no flicker; apps open; no invisible strip above the dock blocks clicks (known for some styles, B-15).
8. **Voice** — hold Right Ctrl and say "minimise this", then "undo".
   **Expected:** the focused window minimises and comes back; nothing is typed into other apps unless you asked for dictation.
9. **Lens Command after a fresh start** — restart Lens, press Ctrl+Alt+Space at once and type "dock style floating".
   **Expected:** the very first command works (this used to get lost).
10. **Core apps** — open Timer with `/timer` in Command, start 1 minute and pin it with the pin button in its title bar.
    **Expected:** the dock shows the countdown on the Timer icon and the alert fires once.
11. **Lens Snip recording** — start a short screen recording from Lens Snip, trim it and save.
    **Expected:** the file plays; the Lens at work orb and the Snip toolbar are not in the recording.
12. **Windows 10** (if you have a Windows 10 PC) — run the setup wizard and turn on the dock.

## Known issues

- **Agent safety is not yet wired into every agent path** (B-04, critical). The Settings card works, but Command plans, voice and the browser agent don't all go through it yet. Don't point the agent at untrusted pages.
- **Opt-in window-style hook** (Settings › Windows, "injected hook") loads a DLL into every app and an updated DLL isn't reinstalled; Remove all doesn't stop it until you restart (B-05, B-06). Leave it off unless you're testing it.
- **Dock window sizing:** some styles clip or have dead click space above the icons (B-15).
- **Esc doesn't stop Lens at work unless a Lens window has focus**; use Ctrl+Alt+Pause (B-13).
- **Magic menu isn't reachable from the dock, Shelf or notifications** yet (B-11).
- **Remove all doesn't undo Lens Apps recipes** applied to installed apps (B-08).
- **The Windows November update (KB5132061) reportedly broke some Windhawk mods.** If you use Lens's Windhawk extras, update Windhawk first. Not verified by us.
- Bug IDs come from `docs/brain/BUGS.md` (22 Nov); some may have moved since.
