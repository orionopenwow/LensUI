## Open Lens Cleaner
show: settings:storage

Settings › Storage has Lens Cleaner at the top. You can also type /clean in Lens Command or say "Lens, free up space". It only looks until you say otherwise: nothing is deleted until you click.

## Scan, then read the list
show: settings:storage

Scan my PC takes a few seconds. Caches and temporary files are marked SAFE and ticked for you. Your own files (big downloads, old installers, duplicates, games) say YOUR CALL and are never ticked.

## Check the preview
show: settings:storage

Clean up shows a dry run first: every file that would be deleted, everything that goes to the Recycle Bin, and anything Windows will ask permission for. Smart Clean does the same with SAFE items only.

## Changed your mind?
show: settings:storage

Anything that went to the Recycle Bin can come back: press Restore in the toast right after cleaning, or open the Recycle Bin later. Every clean is written to the cleaner log.

## See where the space went
show: settings:storage

The Space map shows every folder as a list, a treemap or a sunburst, with the biggest files on the side. Turn on daily snapshots to see which folders grew this week.
